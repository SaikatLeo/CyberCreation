STEP 01 The Spark

Early PC Gaming Roots & Classic Comics

Grew up hooked on classic PC games and legendary comic-book storytelling (from epic adventures like Chacha Chaudhary to open-world action), sparking a lifelong obsession with virtual worlds, tech hardware, and creative design.

STEP 02 Page Launch

Birth of Cyber Creation

Founded the CYBER CREATION gaming page and online identity—crafting custom esports visuals, stream intros, gaming memes, and gathering an energetic Bangladeshi community around daily gameplay.

GTA V FiveM Roleplay & Qbox Dev

Immersed into Bangladeshi GTA V RP culture on Generation Roleplay (GRP) as Cyber, while diving under the hood to experiment with the modern Qbox (qbx) server framework, gang logos, and custom RP assets.

STEP 04 Official Collab

Teamed up with Z-Core Bangladesh to power live-stream promotions, exclusive gamer offers, and high-hype Discord community giveaways rewarding loyal CYBER CREATION supporters.

STEP 05 // 2026+ Expanding
